SandFall — Privacy Policy

Developer: Orion Soft Games · Last updated: October 4, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game SandFall ("the app") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect ourselves

We do not collect personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no chat. Your game progress (scores, coins, purchased color palettes, daily quests, statistics and settings) is stored only on your device and is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or using "Reset progress" in Settings deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and interactions with ads. This is handled by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Rewarded ads are optional: you choose to watch one to continue a game.

3. Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalized ads are shown. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time by reinstalling the app or by resetting your advertising ID in your device settings.

4. Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).
  • Opt out of personalized ads in the same Android settings menu.
  • Delete all game data: Settings → Reset progress, or uninstall the app.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Saved images

If you tap "Save image" on the score card, the picture is saved to your device's Pictures folder. It is not uploaded anywhere by the app.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
